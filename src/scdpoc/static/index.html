EHDS Structured Clinical Document PoC

Preserve the clinical document.
Exchange the computable summary.

Follow one synthetic patient summary from source data, through an HL7 FHIR International Patient Summary, into a PDF/A-3b preservation envelope, through IHE XDS.b and MHD sharing, and into an EHDS-oriented export preview - then across a simulated border to a clinician in a second jurisdiction. Every step shows the artefact and its evidence.

OpenAPI FHIR CapabilityStatement
    1

    Choose a synthetic patient

    Source facts from a fixed fixture path. Identifiers are in the 2.999 example OID arc and must start with SYN-.

    2

    Compose the IPS

    Map source data to an HL7 FHIR IPS document Bundle: Composition first, sections, profiles and terminology.

    3

    Validate

    Offline pre-flight plus the HL7 FHIR validator against the pinned IPS package (when configured). Errors block publication.

    4

    Render

    The human-readable summary is generated from the same IPS instance - never the other way round.

    5

    Package the preservation envelope

    PDF/A-3b with the exact validated IPS bytes embedded as an Associated File (AFRelationship=Source).

    6

    Validate the preservation format

    Generation is not conformance. veraPDF is the independent validator; the built-in pre-flight is a subset check only.

    7

    Publish through XDS.b

    Two ITI-41 submissions: the envelope (preserved package), then the IPS projection linked to it by an XFRM association.

    8

    Discover and retrieve

    The same registry content seen through SOAP (ITI-18 / ITI-43) and FHIR (ITI-67 / ITI-68), then checked for integrity.

    9

    Exchange projection

    A consumer obtains application/fhir+json directly - no PDF unpacking.

    10

    EHDS export preview

    What can be shown now, and what remains controlled by implementing acts, national rules or MyHealth@EU onboarding. Non-normative.

    XB

    Cross-border: a clinician in Jurisdiction B reads the summary

    Simulated second jurisdiction (de-DE) with its own patient index. It reaches Jurisdiction A only through A's responding gateway: IHE XCPD (ITI-55) and XCA (ITI-38 / ITI-39) over HTTP. Publish a patient in A first.

    +

    Replace, audit and attempt to tamper

    Issued documents are immutable. A new version replaces the current one through an RPLC association; the old snapshot stays retrievable.
